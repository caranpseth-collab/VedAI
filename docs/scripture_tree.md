# 🌿 VedAI Scripture & Branch Taxonomy Map (Veda Shakhas)

This documentation serves as the comprehensive data-parsing blueprint for onboarding specialized downstream Rishi Agents. It maps the four primary Veda structures, their surviving active branches (*Shakhas*), and their structural text splits into the **Nalanda Library Core Index**.

## 🧭 1. The Rigveda Structural Tree
* **Core Attribute:** Foundational elemental physics, cosmological constants, and wave metric formulas.
* **Active Branch (Shakha):** 
  * **Shakala Shakha:** The primary surviving textual structure containing 10 Mandalas and 1,028 Suktas.
* **Component Layer Splits:**
  * **Samhita Layer:** Rigveda Samhita (Mantric frequency strings).
  * **Brahmana Layer:** Aitareya Brahmana & Kaushitaki Brahmana (Ritual/Operational mechanics).
  * **Aranyaka Layer:** Aitareya Aranyaka & Shankhayana Aranyaka (Metaphysical transitions).
  * **Upanishad Layer:** Aitareya Upanishad (Consciousness observer mechanics).

## ⚡ 2. The Yajurveda Structural Tree
* **Core Attribute:** Algorithmic logic loops, operational execution workflows, and kinetic actions.
* **Main Divisions:**

### A. Shukla Yajurveda (White / Organized Corpus)
* **Active Branches (Shakhas):**
  * **Madhyandina (Vajasaneyi) Shakha:** Widely implemented across Northern geopolitical lines.
  * **Kanva Shakha:** Preserved primarily across Southern geopolitical lines.
* **Component Layer Splits:**
  * **Samhita Layer:** Vajasaneyi Samhita (40 Adhyayas).
  * **Brahmana Layer:** Shatapatha Brahmana (100 Chapters—Extensive historical/geographical coordinates).
  * **Upanishad Layer:** Isha Upanishad & Brihadaranyaka Upanishad (Deep non-dual logical proofs).

### B. Krishna Yajurveda (Black / Interwoven Corpus)
* **Active Branches (Shakhas):**
  * **Taittiriya Shakha:** Heavily structured; forms the basis of foundational cognitive mapping workflows.
  * **Maitrayani Shakha** & **Kathaka Shakha** (Esoteric branch streams).
* **Component Layer Splits:**
  * **Samhita & Brahmana Layer:** Taittiriya Samhita & Taittiriya Brahmana.
  * **Upanishad Layer:** Taittiriya Upanishad, Katha Upanishad, and Shvetashvatara Upanishad.

## 🎵 3. The Samaveda Structural Tree
* **Core Attribute:** Acoustic resonance metrics, structural sound wave physics, and frequency intervals.
* **Active Branches (Shakhas):**
  * **Kauthuma Shakha:** The most prominent surviving musical notation structure.
  * **Jaiminiya (Talavakara) Shakha** & **Ranayaniya Shakha**.
* **Component Layer Splits:**
  * **Samhita Layer:** Archika & Gana text notation sheets (Acoustic data arrays).
  * **Brahmana Layer:** Jaiminiya Brahmana & Tandya Mahabrahmana.
  * **Upanishad Layer:** Chandogya Upanishad (The cosmology of absolute vibration) & Kena Upanishad.

---

## 🛡️ 4. The Atharvaveda Structural Tree
* **Core Attribute:** Base chemical combinations, environmental thermodynamics, and engineering sciences.
* **Active Branches (Shakhas):**
  * **Shaunaka Shakha:** The core operational manual structure.
  * **Paippalada Shakha:** Highly valuable tracking corpus containing advanced conceptual data profiles.
* **Component Layer Splits:**
  * **Brahmana Layer:** Gopatha Brahmana.
  * **Upanishad Layer:** Mundaka Upanishad, Mandukya Upanishad (Four-state consciousness model), and Prashna Upanishad.
